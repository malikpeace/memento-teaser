Delete your account

Updated October 17, 2026

Memento is made by Malik Peace. You can delete your Memento account and the data we keep for it at any time, in the app or by email. You do not need the app installed to ask.

In the app

  1. Open Memento and go to Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter the code we email you, to confirm it is you.

Your account and synced data are then erased from our servers and from that device.

Without the app

Email privacy@mementoapp.co from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Memento account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, use the email of that account; if you chose Apple's Hide My Email, write from that relay address or tell us what it is. We may need to confirm the request really comes from you, then we delete your account and its data. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Everything you wrote and synced: your goals, plans, actions, notes, answers and settings.
  • Short-term copies of your synced data, kept to undo a bad sync, are removed by scheduled cleanup within 30 days.

What we keep

  • A short receipt that a deletion happened, holding a scrambled code rather than your email or anything you wrote, so we can prove and finish it.
  • Records of purchases, as long as needed for refunds, tax, accounting and fraud prevention. Apple and Polar keep their own records.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage that in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

Delete some data, keep your account

Write to privacy@mementoapp.co to see, correct or delete anything specific without deleting your account.
