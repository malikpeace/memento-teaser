Privacy

Updated October 20, 2026

Memento is a personal app. What you write in it belongs to you. This page says what we collect, why we collect it, which companies help us run Memento, how long we keep things, and how to get your data back or delete it.

The short version

  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share anything with data brokers.
  • What you write is used to run Memento for you, including its AI features, and for nothing else.
  • You can download your data and delete your account inside the app, at any time.
  • Memento does not record your screen.
  • Memento is for adults aged 18 or older.

Who we are

Memento is made by Malik Peace ("we", "us"). For anything on this page, write to privacy@mementoapp.co.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and how you sign in (an email code, Apple, or Google). If you use Apple or Google, they share the identity details needed to sign you in.
  • What you write and do in Memento. Your name and birthday from setup (the birthday powers your life-in-weeks and day counts), your Clarity answers and conversations, including completed conversations, your goals, plans, notes, reflections, check-ins and progress. If you sign in, this syncs to your account so you can restore it on another device.
  • Photos you add. These stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or included in account sync.
  • Screen Time. If you turn on app blocking, your app usage is read by Apple's Screen Time system on your iPhone. That data never leaves your device and we never receive it.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on. A push address for your device, your time zone, your reminder settings, and the limited goal and day details needed to choose the right reminder. Turning notifications off in your phone's settings stops them showing but does not immediately delete the server registration; Memento removes it the next time it runs, or you can turn reminders off inside Memento.
  • Purchases. Your plan, store, transaction and subscription identifiers, status, expiry and refunds. We never receive or store your card number.
  • Usage and crash information. Simple activity events (for example, that a screen was opened or a check-in was made) tied to a random identifier for your installation, and technical crash reports. These do not include your name, email or what you write, though a random identifier is not a promise of complete anonymity. Memento does not record your screen. Earlier test builds sent masked screen recordings with text and images hidden; turning recording off does not erase earlier recordings, which we are deleting.
  • Technical data. Network addresses and device identifiers, used to route requests, apply fair-use limits and stop abuse.
  • Messages you send us. Support and feedback messages, with basic technical context.
  • Reports you send from the app: your words, any photos or videos you attach, and basic technical details (app version, phone model, and a random ID for your install).
  • On our website. If you join the email list, your email goes to our newsletter provider. The website also counts basic page activity.

How we use it

  • To run Memento for you: your account, sync, plans, reminders and AI features. This is what you signed up for.
  • To keep Memento safe: security, fraud and abuse prevention, and fair-use limits.
  • To make Memento better: usage events and crash reports, never the contents of what you write.
  • To handle payments, refunds and the Locked-In Guarantee, and to meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
  • To email you: sign-in codes always; the newsletter only if you join it.

If you are in the EU or UK, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running the app you use), our legitimate interests (security and improving the app, balanced against your rights), your consent (reminders and the newsletter, which you can withdraw at any time), and legal obligations.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text it needs, such as your goals, answers and the conversation so far, is sent through our server to Anthropic or OpenAI to write the reply. Under their commercial API terms, these providers do not use it to train their models, and they may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. AI replies can be wrong. Memento is not medical, mental-health, legal or financial advice.

Background plan building stores its inputs, intermediate passes and result for about 24 hours so the work can finish while the app is closed, then removes them through scheduled cleanup.

Companies that help us run Memento

These providers process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: accounts, database and server functions (servers in the United States).
  • Anthropic and OpenAI: the AI features.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and iPhone notifications. Google: Sign in with Google.
  • Polar: purchases made on our website.
  • PostHog: usage events. Sentry: crash reports.
  • Resend: sign-in code emails. beehiiv: the optional email list.
  • GitHub: reports you send from the app become private issues in our GitHub project, so we can track and fix them.

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it. If Memento is ever sold or merged, your data would move with it under the same promises, and we would tell you first.

Where your data lives

Our servers are in the United States, and our providers may process data there. If you use Memento from another country, your data is transferred to the United States, protected by our providers' standard safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you write: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Recovery copies of your synced data (to undo a bad sync): up to 30 days and a limited number, removed by scheduled cleanup.
  • Background AI work: about 24 hours.
  • Error reports: about 90 days.
  • Reports stay in GitHub as our support record, including your words, even after you delete your account. Photos and videos are deleted 90 days after the report is filed, or when you delete your account, whichever comes first. If you want a report removed, write to us.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for refunds, tax, accounting and fraud prevention. Apple and Polar keep their own records.
  • After deleting your account: a short receipt that a deletion happened, holding a scrambled code rather than your email or anything you wrote, so we can prove and finish it.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data in the app at any time. A download covers your Memento data; it is not a complete export of every record our providers keep, so ask us for anything else.
  • Correct what you wrote directly in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app. After an email code confirms it is you, your account and synced data are erased from our servers and from that device. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage that in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. Full steps, including how to ask without the app: Delete your account.
  • Turn off reminders in Memento and the newsletter with its unsubscribe link.
  • Ask us at privacy@mementoapp.co to see, correct or delete anything else, including support reports not linked to an account, which we cannot match to you automatically. We reply within 30 days and may need to confirm the request really comes from you.

California residents have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

EU and UK residents also have the right to restrict or object to our use of their data, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to their local data protection authority.

Security

Data travels encrypted, each account can only reach its own data, sign-in keys are kept in your iPhone's secure Keychain, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Memento is for people 18 or older and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Sensitive topics

You may write about your health, money or relationships. We treat everything you write as private and use it only to provide Memento. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number; in the United States, you can call or text 988.

Public share links and the Locked-In Guarantee

If you create a share link on the website, anyone with that link can see what you chose to share until you delete your account; copies posted elsewhere are controlled by that platform. For the Locked-In Guarantee, completing your main Action sends a proof identifier, the completion time and your time-zone offset, used only to count completion days, never the text of your goal or Action.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and update the date above.
